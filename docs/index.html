Link your game

    
Pick your 3DXChat Game folder (the one with 3DXChat_Data in it), e.g. Downloads\3DXChatLauncherV6\3DXChat\Game. The editor reads the game's models and textures on this PC — nothing is uploaded, even if your browser's prompt says "upload".

    
Inside the SBS window your browser may ask for the 3DXChat_Data folder itself instead. Textures stored in the game's "crunched" format keep their average colour here; the desktop app shows all of them.
